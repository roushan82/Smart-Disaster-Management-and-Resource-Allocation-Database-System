Metric Definitions

  • Waiting Time: Time spent waiting in the ready queue before allocation processing begins or resumes ($T_{\text{turnaround}} - T_{\text{duration}}$). Includes initial queue delay and all interleaved wait intervals between preemption slices in Round Robin.
  • Turnaround Time: Total elapsed time from request arrival/creation to full allocation completion ($T_{\text{completion}} - T_{\text{arrival}}$).
  • Response Time: Time elapsed from request arrival until the scheduler first dispatches or attempts the request ($T_{\text{first\_start}} - T_{\text{arrival}}$). For preemptive Round Robin, response time reflects initial time-slice dispatch.
  • Throughput: Number of successfully completed allocations divided by the total observation window ($\text{Completed Requests} / \Delta t$). Expressed in requests per hour (req/h) and requests per second.
